YOU HAVE BEEN TOLD · PSORIASIS

First, understand what that means.

Psoriasis is not simply dry skin. The skin is receiving inflammatory instructions that keep its renewal cycle switched on too strongly.

IL-17A HAS A NORMAL JOB

It is not the enemy.
Your body needs it.

IL-17A helps barrier tissues call for antimicrobial defence and recruit immune cells when protection is needed. In psoriasis, the signalling loop is dysregulated.

MAIN TREATMENT · IXEKIZUMAB / COPELLOR

Block one signal.
Quiet the loop.

Ixekizumab binds IL-17A so it cannot activate its receptor normally. That reduces one major inflammatory instruction reaching the skin.

THE SAME ACTION HAS TWO CONSEQUENCES

Benefit and biological cost
must be seen together.

WHAT WE WANT

Reduce the psoriasis signal

Less IL-17A receptor activation can reduce plaque-driving inflammation and abnormal skin activity.

WHAT ELSE CHANGES

Normal defence is also affected

IL-17A also participates in barrier and mucosal defence. This is why infection and candidiasis belong in the safety conversation.

THE WORLD DOES NOT HAVE ONE PSORIASIS TREATMENT

There are many treatment families.

ILMB groups them by route and mechanism so options can be compared without pretending they are equivalent.
