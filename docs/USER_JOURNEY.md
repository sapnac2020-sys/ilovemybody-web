# I Love My Body — Patient Journey

## What the hospital operates on

I Love My Body is a self-care hospital that works alongside existing medical treatment. It does not replace diagnosis, prescribing, emergency care, or a patient's qualified clinician.

The operating cycle is:

1. **Medical starting point** — current reports, prescriptions, medicines, procedures and lived symptoms.
2. **Connected starting point** — ten guided assessment days covering Love, Instinct, Fear, Physical, Sexual, Emotional, Relational, Mental, Occupational and Money.
3. **Personal direction** — identify what the person wants to understand or change and agree on observable measures.
4. **Seven-week journey** — record small actions, daily experience, body signals and relevant measurements.
5. **Two-day review** — repeat comparable assessments and reports, compare like with like and prepare questions for clinicians.
6. **Decision** — finish, maintain, or begin a second seven-week journey with a revised focus.

The backend may compute patterns and hypotheses. The patient sees plain language, sources and boundaries—not a diagnosis generated by software.

## Completion rule

Every stage must show:

- **Where am I?**
- **Why am I doing this?**
- **What is my one next action?**
- **What counts as complete?**
- **What happens after completion?**

Only one action is primary at a time.

## Continuous entrance

### Scene 1 — Universe

The screen contains only an animated universe and a pulsating heart. Selecting the heart begins the experience.

### Scene 2 — World

Earth appears. The camera travels towards the I Love My Body hospital dome. This is a transition, not a page requiring another decision.

### Scene 3 — Lobby

The visitor arrives in a wide hospital lobby. A pulsating heart at reception is the single invitation to approach the counter.

### Scene 4 — Reception

Reception asks: **What brings you here?**

The visitor can choose one or more:

1. I have an illness.
2. I feel anxious, tense or stressed.
3. I am struggling with addiction.
4. I feel lost and seek direction.

These are entrances, not labels.

### Scene 5 — First relevant questions

Reception asks no more than three questions at once. Questions change with the chosen entrance. The visitor can skip, pause or return.

### Scene 6 — Registration

Registration appears only after the visitor chooses to continue:

- name;
- age;
- mobile number;
- OTP or PIN;
- preferred language;
- privacy and consent.

The person receives a private hospital record and enters My Happy Space.

## My Happy Space

My Happy Space is one dashboard—not a collection of competing pages.

The dashboard contains:

1. a compact journey line;
2. the current stage;
3. one explanation of why it matters;
4. one primary action;
5. a clear completion requirement;
6. a preview of what comes next;
7. quiet secondary access to records, journal, check-in and preferences.

## Patient stages

### Stage 0 — Registration

**Complete when:** identity, consent and privacy setup are saved.

### Stage 1 — Medical picture

**Action:** add available reports, prescriptions, medicines, procedures and current concerns.

**Complete when:** the person confirms that the available starting information has been added. Missing information is permitted and visibly recorded as missing.

### Stage 2 — Ten-day assessment

**Action:** complete one short guided assessment each day.

**Complete when:** all required safety questions and the minimum assessment set are answered. Optional questions remain optional.

### Stage 3 — Connected summary

**Action:** review strengths, pressures, possible connections, missing information, sources and boundaries.

**Complete when:** the person acknowledges the summary and selects a journey focus.

### Stage 4 — First seven weeks

**Action:** complete the day's single suggested action and optionally record check-in, journal, body signals, food, medicines and measurements.

**Complete when:** the scheduled period ends. Missed days do not erase progress.

### Stage 5 — Two-day review

**Action:** repeat relevant assessments, upload new reports where available, compare like with like and prepare clinician questions.

**Complete when:** the comparison and next-step decision are recorded.

### Stage 6 — Finish or continue

The person may finish, maintain independently, or start a second seven-week journey. A second journey is never automatic.

## Interface rules

- Dashboard body text: 14–16 px on desktop and 15–17 px on mobile.
- Display headings: never exceed 40 px on desktop or 30 px on mobile.
- No giant editorial headlines inside the patient workspace.
- No more than one primary CTA per screen.
- No unexplained icons.
- No medical or psychological labels generated from self-report answers.
- Previewing the journey does not require login; saving private information does.
- The public entrance, lobby and patient area must feel like one continuous hospital experience.
