# Weight-loss process comparison — first extraction
Date: 2026-09-30. Research record, not a self-treatment protocol.

## Purpose
For the same outcome, compare each modality's actual sourced actions, proposed targets, proposed bodily effects and measured results. The ILMB hospital is a self-help and self-healing model without practitioners delivering sessions. A practitioner-dependent study can inform research but cannot be converted automatically into a self-practice offering.

## Outcome identity
Weight loss (mass), waist reduction (circumference) and fat loss (body composition) are distinct outcomes. Do not merge them. Preserve duration and measurement method in every comparison.

## Study appraisal
Renjhen P, Jha RP, Thirani S. Efficacy of pranic healing for inch-loss. Journal of Preventive Medicine and Holistic Health. 2023;9(2):103–105. DOI: 10.18231/j.jpmhh.2023.018.

The accessible abstract describes 49 volunteers, a 60-minute distant session and immediate waist measurements. It reports a decrease in more than 90% of participants. No causal effect estimate is extracted here. Publisher-indexed Table 3 lists pre/post medians of 39 and 38 inches; a difference of group medians is not the median individual change. The publisher-indexed limitations state that durability was not examined. A displayed p=0.000 must not be interpreted as zero probability.

Publisher PDF retrieval failed. On 2026-09-30 the author-linked ResearchGate full-text record was reviewed: sections 3.1–3.2 explicitly describe self-reported waist measurements and no control group. It does not specify the exact healing sequence or a standardised tape-measure procedure. This closes the earlier methods-retrieval gap using the paper itself; publisher-version matching remains pending. No raw paired data, blinded measurement audit, registration or correction/retraction audit has been completed. The design cannot isolate an energy-specific mechanism from the information verified so far.

## Measurement contract
Record weight, waist and body composition separately. Capture device, measurement site, posture, respiratory phase, clothing, time of day, repeated readings, follow-up duration, hydration, meals, activity, medications and concurrent practices where relevant to the research question.

Use only existing approved LOINC identities for compatible observations. Preserve units and the raw values. No code is guessed for a chakra, a session or a fat-loss claim. Research variables and process-step labels are local identifiers, not medical terminology codes.

## Pattern rules
Compare ordered actions before grouping modalities. Keep each original tradition's target label; proposed biological correspondences must be separate links with their own evidence. A shared word such as energy does not establish a shared physical quantity. Repeated sources from one teaching lineage do not count as independent corroboration.

A candidate pattern requires traceable matching processes or observations across independently sourced records. A supported bodily mechanism requires separate causal evidence. Report contradictory findings and missing edges alongside matches.
