# Energy healing research scope and evidence register

Research date: 30 September 2026. Scope: the 50 existing catalogue identities, with separate treatment of branches, families and delivery styles. This is an in-progress evidence map; no global exhaustiveness or clinical validation is claimed.

## Inclusion and extraction rules

- Retain original empirical human, animal, plant/cell and measurement studies identifying the named practice. Keep their populations and outcomes separate.
- Retain reviews for context and citation chasing, without counting them as independent replications.
- Exclude conventional devices, surgery, molecular biology and unrelated psychological coding that merely share a name.
- Do not attribute a parent practice to a branch, infer an unspecified lineage, or treat a combined intervention as an isolated energy effect.
- Distinguish randomized allocation from rotating recruitment-order assignment; within-group change from a controlled contrast; clinical endpoints from surrogate markers; and human from preclinical findings.
- Record exact query and translation, date cutoff, returned identifiers, screening level, sample/attrition, comparator/masking, effects/uncertainty, disclosures, notices and available procedure. A retained title is not an appraised full text.
- No study is rejected just because its result is null or negative. Uncontrolled and qualitative reports describe associations or experiences.

[Registry correspondence ledger](research/energy-healing-registry-checks.csv): six current ClinicalTrials.gov records examined, four discrepancies and two partial matches. Current records are not proof of original prespecification; historical versions, amendments, statistical plans and publisher updates remain open.

- NCT05483842: first submission/posting after completion; registry enrollment 66 versus abstract 65; seven primary outcomes require multiplicity review.
- NCT03696056: posted after listed start, registry enrollment 36 versus paper randomized 31; published biomarker panel needs historical reconciliation. Cognitive/biomarker papers share one cohort.
- NCT04766242: feasibility is primary, depression scores secondary; status is stale. Generic spiritual-healing pilot is not a confirmed Spiritualist intervention.
- NCT00288795: registry SINGLE_GROUP field conflicts with the three-arm report; fatigue remains the primary outcome and the published BFI comparison nonsignificant.
- NCT04632368: current primary BSI-18 stress outcome corresponds broadly to the TM report; no significant primary benefit. One-month app-related missing data and unadjusted secondary testing were confirmed in full Methods/Results.

## Key changes in interpretation

- Reconnective Healing shoulder mobility: full Methods describe rotating recruitment-order allocation. The study is nonrandomized; pain reduction was comparable with sham.
- Polarity Therapy fatigue: the primary BFI comparison was nonsignificant. Favorable diary findings were secondary; baseline imbalance and exploratory analyses matter.
- Healing Touch chemoradiation: immune-marker differences did not establish cancer survival benefit; toxicity and treatment-delay differences were nonsignificant.
- Pranic diabetic foot: corrected results and funding were used. Revised prose/tables still show inconsistent wound-change/sample values; raw-data reconciliation is open.
- Heartfulness psoriasis: primary PASI 75 difference was nonsignificant (p=0.548); no psoriasis cure claim.
- Biofield Tuning: the quantitative and qualitative feasibility papers are overlapping reports of one cohort, not independent efficacy replications. A separate inter-rater study found poor agreement.

Additional abstract findings include null Reiki physiology results (PMID33639516), no TT advantage for carpal tunnel or breast biopsy (11572538/17661855), unfavorable Healing Touch fatigue results (24105358), failed external-qi cell replication (15102336), null Johrei cell/radiation results (15667653/17549235), and a nonsignificant primary TM distress outcome despite favorable secondary outcomes (36121655). Combined packages, within-arm improvements, surrogate measurements and probable repeated cohort reports remain distinct from isolated clinical efficacy.

## What remains before full research completion

Screen expanded results; independently resolve eligible primary texts and lineage; assess bias and publication/reporting bias; match trial registries and prespecified endpoints; check publisher/Crossmark updates beyond PubMed links; include regional/non-English databases and citation chasing; extract complete outcome-specific sequences where available; synthesize each population/outcome without inappropriate pooling. No new clinical experiments have been performed.

Source access limitations remain explicit. Several publisher pages expose abstracts and a paid full-article link. A PubMed or PMC identifier alone is not proof that an accessible full article body was read. Scientific validation may remain inconclusive even after literature-review tasks are complete.
