## Claim interpretation
The spiritual origin and training are documented organisation teachings. Wellbeing, distant effects, DNA/RNA recoding and a physical laser mechanism require distinct evidence. Evidence from another energy-healing practice cannot be transferred automatically. Sacred geometry is a teaching concept; a diagram or mathematical equation alone does not validate biological effects.

## Verification designs stored in the database
- MH_O01: first-phase wellbeing outcome against matched attention. Define one validated scale, dose, edition and four-week endpoint. Calculate power from independent variance and a meaningful effect. Conceal randomisation, mask assessors and analysts, preserve usual care, and analyse by allocation.
- MH_D01: concealed distant sessions against sham. No recipient-practitioner contact; recipients and assessors are unaware of assignment and timing. Freeze the endpoint, window and missing-data plan; account for repeated measurements.
- MH_M01: one quantitatively predicted physical signal. State quantity, magnitude, location, bandwidth and detection threshold before instrumentation. Randomise active/sham periods, mask operator and analyst, and include movement and drift controls.

All three protocols are DRAFT with no registration, ethics record or results. They are not public recruitment notices. Molecular recoding is not an executable study until a specific molecular alteration, assay, tissue and biological prediction are defined with qualified investigators. Do not take invasive samples based on a spiritual phrase.

Use the statistical and calibration requirements in [Pranic verification protocol](pranic-healing-verification-protocol.md), adapting the intervention rather than assuming equivalence. Clinical or veterinary enrolment requires appropriate oversight and applicable ethics approval.
