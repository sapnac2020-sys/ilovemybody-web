# UCUM canonical synchronization contract

Status: authoritative UCUM 2.2 atomic prefixes and units are registered for validation and staging. Production promotion remains approval and reconciliation controlled.

## Canonical language

- The official case-sensitive UCUM `Code` is the canonical unit code.
- The official `CODE` is retained separately for explicitly case-insensitive consumers.
- Names and print symbols are display values, never identity keys.
- Prefix, base-unit, and defined-unit records remain distinct source classes.
- Compound UCUM expressions are generated by the UCUM grammar; this module does not pretend to enumerate an infinite expression space.
- Special units require the named source conversion function and must not be reduced to a simple multiplier.
- Source presence is not a clinical reference interval, diagnostic threshold, treatment rule, or release approval.

## Formula boundary

Every promoted ILMB formula must declare each variable's canonical UCUM code, expected dimension, input/output role, allowed context, source, uncertainty, and test cases. Unit compatibility is necessary for a formula; it does not establish clinical validity.

## Deployment boundary

The worker validates and stages the workbook. Promotion requires explicit approval plus count/hash reconciliation. Raw workbooks remain private and do not belong in `public_html`.
