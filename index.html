About I Love My Body

Discover your magic. Find your happiness.

I Love My Body is building an evidence-mapped non-invasive online hospital: a private place that connects medical records with daily food, feelings, body signals, choices, actions, environments and measured responses.

It was created from Sapna Jain Chaturvedi’s lived experience, long inquiry into food and human behaviour, and determination to examine an interconnected philosophy honestly through science and personal longitudinal evidence.

Happiness now. Illness never.

This is an intention and direction—not a guarantee of cure. The platform complements appropriate medical care and does not replace emergency, diagnostic or prescribing services.

Vision

Every person can understand their own connected life.

Make credible knowledge, careful observation and non-invasive support accessible without erasing uncertainty.
